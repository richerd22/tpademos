Wolfie's All Natural Treats

Small-batch dog treats, hand-made in South Boston with ingredients you can pronounce. Named for a German Shepherd people kept mistaking for a wolf.

The story

It started with Leyna.

Jane Hayes Handrahan started making treats at home for her German Shepherd, Leyna, because she wanted to know exactly what was going into them. Enough people mistook Leyna for a wolf that the name took care of itself.

Leyna gave the business its name. Mina is the one you will spot on the packaging.

What began in a home kitchen now runs on three industrial dehydrators, with treats made almost every day. It has stayed a South Boston business the whole way - built on repeat customers and word of mouth rather than shelf space in a chain.
