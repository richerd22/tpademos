Tom English's Cottage

A real Southie neighborhood bar - cold beer at fair prices, the game on, and a crowd that mixes people who have been coming for decades with people who just moved in.

The bar

No frills, on purpose.

Guinness on draught, cheap cold beer, darts, pool, and whatever game matters that day on the TVs - the regulars will tell you that covers everything a bar actually needs.

Cash only, and there is an ATM inside.
